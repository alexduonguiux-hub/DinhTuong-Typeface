Dinh Tuong is a display typeface drawn from the cast-steel stencil lettering of the shop signs and gate signs of 1960s Saigon. Every part is built with ruler and compass only: straight stems with a slight brush flare at their free ends, rounds that are upright ellipses, and open terminals that end in one vertical cut.

The Vietnamese marks are not added on top of the letters: they are cut from the same steel strip, in the same stroke system, so a page of Vietnamese text keeps one even colour. Stacked marks share one top line, and every pair is kerned against the ink of its marks.

The family comes in three weights, Regular, Medium and Bold, and supports Vietnamese, the GF Latin Core character set, and tabular figures.

To contribute, see github.com/alexduonguiux-hub/DinhTuong-Typeface.
